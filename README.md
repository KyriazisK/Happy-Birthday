# ✨ Happy Birthday Irene ✨

A bespoke, starlit birthday website crafted for Irene. Built with modern HTML5, CSS3 3D animations, canvas starry sky particles, and the Web Audio API.

## 🌟 Interactive Experience Flow

1. **The Wax-Sealed Vintage Envelope**:
   - Features a glowing monogrammed wax seal with "I" for Irene.
   - Tap or click to crack the seal, unfold the 3D envelope flap, and reveal the celebration inside.
   - Initiates celebratory confetti and plays the birthday tune.

2. **The 3D Birthday Cake & Candles**:
   - A multi-layered celebration cake with Irene's name.
   - Realistic flickering candle flames with glowing ambient light.
   - Irene can tap the individual candles or click the "Blow Out Candles" button to make a wish, triggering smoke drift effects and a grand celebration confetti cascade!

3. **The Upbeat Birthday Card**:
   - A clean, modern celebratory card wishing her an awesome birthday full of great food, good music, lots of laughs, and plenty of cake.
   - Lighthearted, friendly, and zero emotional pressure.

4. **Interactive Celebration Controls**:
   - Interactive buttons on the card to throw more confetti, release clusters of colorful floating balloons, and toggle music.

5. **Aesthetic Vinyl Music Player**:
   - Floating widget at the top-right corner.
   - Spins smoothly with tonearm animation when playing.
   - **Zero-dependency Music**: Automatically plays a soothing chime music box rendition of *Happy Birthday* using the browser's native Web Audio API (no external MP3 hosting or CORS issues).
   - **Custom Song Support**: If you want to use a specific song (e.g., an artist or track you both liked), simply drop an audio file named `song.mp3` into this directory, and the player will automatically detect and play it!

## 🚀 How to Enable GitHub Pages

Once committed and pushed to GitHub:

1. Open your repository on GitHub: [KyriazisK/Happy-Birthday](https://github.com/KyriazisK/Happy-Birthday)
2. Go to **Settings** (tab at the top right of the repo).
3. In the left sidebar, click **Pages** (under the "Code and automation" section).
4. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`
   - **Branch**: `main`, folder: `/ (root)`
5. Click **Save**.
6. Within 1–2 minutes, your site will be live at:
   👉 **`https://kyriazisk.github.io/Happy-Birthday/`**

## 🛠️ Local Preview

To preview the website locally on your computer:
1. Double-click [index.html](file:///c:/Users/kyria/Desktop/Happy-Birthday/index.html) in your file manager to open it in your web browser.
2. Or run a local Python HTTP server:
   ```bash
   python -m http.server 8000
   ```
   Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
